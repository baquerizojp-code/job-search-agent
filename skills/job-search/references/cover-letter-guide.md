# Cover letter (milestone 5)

For `apply`: one page, four short paragraphs, in the posting's language. 1) The role and one sentence on why this company. 2) The two or three strongest matches, each tied to a result from the CV. 3) Remote fit: time zone overlap, async habits, distributed experience. 4) Availability and a plain closing. No flattery, no repetition of the CV, no em dashes. Save as `cv/tailored/<company>-<role>/cover-letter.md`.
