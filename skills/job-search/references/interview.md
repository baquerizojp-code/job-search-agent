# Onboarding interview

Goal: enough signal to write `profile.md` and `rubric.md` without guessing. Read the CV first. Ask one question at a time, in the workspace language, and accept short answers. Do not ask what the CV already answers; confirm it in one line instead ("Your CV says you have been remote since 2021, correct?").

## Fixed questions (always, in this order)

1. **Target roles.** Which role titles are you going for? Name the ones you would take tomorrow and the ones you would consider.
2. **Roles to avoid.** Which titles or kinds of work do you not want, even if the match looks good?
3. **Location and remote.** Where do you live, which time zones can you cover, and is hybrid or on-site acceptable anywhere?
4. **Work authorization.** Where can you legally work as an employee, and are you open to contractor arrangements elsewhere?
5. **Compensation.** What is your target and your hard floor (say the period: month or year, and the currency)?
6. **Dealbreakers.** Company types, industries, or practices you refuse (crypto, gambling, agencies, unpaid trials, …).
7. **Skills the CV undersells.** Anything you are good at that the CV does not show, and anything on the CV you no longer want to do.
8. **Company stage and domain.** Startup, scale-up, enterprise? Which product domains excite you most, and which are fine but not a goal?

## Extra questions (at most 5, only when the CV leaves a real gap)

Ask only if you cannot fill a profile section after the eight above. Typical gaps:

- Years of experience are unclear (overlapping dates, freelancing).
- Seniority target (IC vs lead vs manager).
- Languages and level, if the CV does not state them.
- Proof of remote or async work, if the CV never mentions it.
- A skill cluster you cannot tag (Expert vs Exposure) because the CV only name-drops it.
- Notice period or earliest start date, if the user mentioned urgency.

## Closing

Summarize the profile in five lines, ask for one correction round, then write `profile.md` and `rubric.md`. Tell the user they can change either file by hand at any time, or run `cv refresh` later to re-ask one dimension.
